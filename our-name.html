Our Name

More than a name on the door.

Every company carries a name. Ours carries a family.

IdaKay Transportation is named after our daughter — her middle name is IdaKay, and this company is being built in her honor.

The name itself is a combination of two women who shaped our family long before she was born. Ida was my wife's paternal grandmother. Kay was the sister who helped raise my wife's father. Two women, two generations back, whose care and presence carried forward into the family we have today.

"IdaKay" isn't just a name we liked the sound of — it's a way of keeping two women who mattered to this family present in something we're building for the next generation.

When it came time to name this company, there wasn't really another option. Building a business is a way of building something that outlasts any one person — and naming it after our daughter, using a name that already honors two women who came before her, felt like the right way to start.

So when you see the IdaKay name on a truck, a trailer, or an invoice, it's not just a brand. It's Ida. It's Kay. And it's our daughter, whose name they became — carried forward, mile after mile.
