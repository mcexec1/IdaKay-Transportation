Services / Over-The-Road

Long-haul freight, coast to coast.

Full truckload and dedicated OTR capacity for shippers who need distance covered, on schedule, without switching carriers mid-route.

What It Is

Over-The-Road service covers long-distance freight moves — typically 250+ miles, running from origin straight through to destination with our own equipment. No relay handoffs, no split loads unless the freight calls for it.

Who It's For

  • 3PLs and freight brokers needing dedicated OTR capacity
  • Direct shippers and manufacturers moving full truckloads long-distance
  • Businesses that need one carrier of record for a lane, not a rotating pool
